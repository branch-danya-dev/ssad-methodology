# 01 · Foundation

[Русская версия](README.ru.md)

This section explains **what SSAD is, why it exists, and which principles support the approach**.

## Main question

> Why should system knowledge be structured around the real system and its responsibilities instead of a universal list of document types?

## Core model

```text
SYSTEM
  ↓
BOUNDARIES
  ↓
RESPONSIBILITIES
  ↓
OWNERSHIP
  ↓
LOCAL KNOWLEDGE
  ↓
CONNECTIONS
  ↓
SYSTEM VIEW
```

It introduces responsibility boundaries, canonical knowledge ownership, hierarchical storage plus graph-like links, local complexity, and the rule that important ideas are explained through theory, method, example and diagram.
