# Selective Reopening

After Change Surface and compatibility analysis, the practical question is:

> **Which previously stable knowledge areas must be reopened, and which can remain unchanged?**

SSAD does not require revalidating the entire system after every change.

Instead, reopen only canonical claims that may no longer be true.

```text
CHANGE SURFACE
      ↓
AFFECTED CLAIMS
      ↓
CANONICAL OWNERS
      ↓
REOPEN NECESSARY KNOWLEDGE
      ↓
REVALIDATE
      ↓
STABILIZE
```

Reopening knowledge does not necessarily mean rewriting a document. It means treating a claim as unconfirmed until it has been validated again.

A simple status model may help:

```text
STABLE
REOPENED
VALIDATING
UPDATED
STABILIZED
```

Knowledge becomes stable again only after sufficient evidence exists: team validation, implementation, verification and canonical knowledge update.

The key principle is:

> **A change reopens claims about the system, not documents.**
